title: Stately Studio Free plan
---

The free plan lets you build and share as many public machines as you like. Pay when you need to work privately, with a team, or keep Studio in sync with your codebase.

<Callout>

You can [upgrade](upgrade) to a [Pro](studio-pro-plan), [Team](studio-team-plan), or [Enterprise](studio-enterprise-plan) plan at any time. Request features and check out what we’ve got planned on [our roadmap](https://feedback.stately.ai).

</Callout>

## Free features

The free plan includes the following:

- Unlimited [public projects](/docs/projects/#change-a-projects-visibility) and machines
- Edit your own machines and projects
- Browse and [simulate](simulate-mode) public machines
- [Discover](discover) public machines
- [Export as code](export-as-code)
- [XState VS Code extension](xstate-vscode-extension)
- Community support on [our Discord](https://discord.stately.ai)

<Callout>

## Why pay for a premium plan?

Our premium plans include private and unlisted projects, codebase sync via GitHub, the CLI, SDK, or MCP server, test path generation, AI assistance with Stately AI, useful features for team management and much more. Your subscription also helps support the Stately team as we build and improve Stately Studio and XState.

</Callout>
